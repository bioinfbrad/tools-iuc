What trackastra is about
- inputs
  - masks - have to show it what is it that you want to track
  - raw - uses AI to score similarity internally
    (not all trackers do it like this!)

- outputs
  - napari tracks aka directly for napari
  - GEFF - IMHO a future format for tracking data exchange
  - CTC format - works the best as of now

Btw, GEFF internally also keeps masks, just like the CTC format.



What if I don't have segmentation masks?

- There's internally also a cellpose3 ('cause for CPU!!)
- it does on-the-fly compute segmentation masks
  - (beware it can be heavy!)
  - and feeds them to trackastra
  - the mask are unf. not saved
    - (to save aside is a feature request, on a TODO list)
